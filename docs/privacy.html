TeamPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Operator: Tinika Ltd (support@tinika.lv)

Summary

TeamPlay is a media player. Tinika Ltd does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from the TeamPlay app for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

This matches the App Store privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected.

What TeamPlay is (and is not)

TeamPlay does not provide, host, sell, or distribute television channels, playlists, movies, or other media content.

You add your own playlist URLs, media-server credentials, or other sources. Playback talks to those endpoints that you configure — not to a Tinika content catalog.

Data stored on your device

Configuration such as playlist URLs, login credentials you enter, favorites, watch progress, and similar settings is stored locally on your device (and, if you enable iCloud features, in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy terms). Tinika does not operate a TeamPlay account server that receives this information.

Purchases

In-app purchases (including Lifetime Access) are processed by Apple. Tinika does not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide Tinika with limited purchase/receipt information needed to unlock premium features, under Apple’s rules.

Optional network features

If you use optional features that call third-party services (for example metadata or subtitle lookups you explicitly use), requests go from your device to those providers under their privacy policies. TeamPlay does not sell that activity and does not build a Tinika-side profile of you from it.

Diagnostic logs you choose to export or email (for example from Settings) leave your device only when you send them.

Children

TeamPlay is not directed at children under 13. Do not provide personal information of children through the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
